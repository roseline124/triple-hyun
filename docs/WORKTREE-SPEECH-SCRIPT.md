# Worktree — 3-minute pitch

**[1. The point]**

Hi, we are Triple Hyun, and this is Worktree.
Worktree helps people who lose work to AI find their next work, and do it together in a guild.
AI is taking jobs fast.
The World Economic Forum says about 92 million jobs will be displaced by 2030.
But 170 million new jobs will be created.
The jobs are there. People need a path to them.
Worktree is that path.

**[2. How it works]**

Worktree has three parts.

First, the skill detector.
You share your work history.
Our AI finds what you can do, including work outside your old field.

Second, the guild.
You join a group of people who do the work you want.
Anyone can start a guild, like an open chat room.
A guild gives you a team, not just a job.

Third, quests.
Clients post work as quests and pay a deposit.
The guild takes the quest and splits the roles.
When the client approves, everyone gets paid, and the guild's reputation grows.

We make money one way: a fee on every completed quest.

**[3. Who it's for]**

Our core users are IT workers. Junior and senior.
Not only people who already lost their jobs.
Also people who still have a job, but feel AI coming.
One of our own developers is one of them.
He has thought about a second career because of AI.

These people have real skills.
They just can't see where else those skills fit.
And outside a company, they have no team.
Worktree gives them both: a next job, and a place to belong.

**[4. Going global]**

We start in the US.
Our demo is built for US users.
According to Upwork, 64 million Americans freelanced in 2023.

We start with online quests, because online work has no borders.
A guild in one country can serve a client in another.
Then local quests grow city by city, wherever guilds form.

Upwork already lets teams work together.
Our difference is where we start: with the person, not the job post.
We find what you can do next. Then we give you a team.

AI will close many doors.
Worktree helps people find the next one, together.

Thank you.
